1. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

All features of ScanPro PDF process data strictly in your device's local memory and private app storage:

  • Scanned Documents & PDFs: Pages captured via camera or imported via the Android Photo Picker / Storage Access Framework (SAF) are stored inside your app's private internal directory (files/documents/) or exported only when you explicitly save or share them.
  • Offline OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Text extraction and searchable PDF generation for English (Latin) and Hindi (Devanagari) run 100% on-device. Recognized text is cached locally on your device so you can search your documents offline and can be cleared anytime in Settings → OCR → Clear cached OCR text.
  • Signatures & Redactions: Drawn or imported signatures are stored inside private app storage. True PDF Redaction permanently strips underlying text and pixels from redacted regions on-device.
  • QR & Barcode Scanner: Scanned QR codes and barcodes are decoded locally. If Save scan history is enabled, history is kept in your local Room database and can be cleared with one tap in Settings → QR & Barcode Scanner.
  • App Preferences & Language Packs: Theme settings, naming templates, and offline language preferences (15 supported languages) are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore.

2. Android Permissions We Use (Least-Privilege Policy)

ScanPro PDF follows Google Play's strict least-privilege permission policy and requests only the permissions required for core functionality:

3. Encryption, App Lock & Hardware Secure Vault

  • Secure Vault (AES-256-GCM): Files moved into the Secure Vault are encrypted on-device using a 256-bit AES-GCM master key generated and stored inside the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • App Lock PIN Hashing: Your App Lock / Vault PIN is never stored in plain text. It is salted with a cryptographic random salt and hashed using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (120,000 iterations).
  • PDF Password Encryption: The Lock PDF tool applies standard AES encryption directly to your PDF file so the password protection travels with the document when shared.
  • Offline Encrypted Backups (.scanpro-backup): When you create a password-protected backup archive, your archive is encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM. Because there is no cloud server or backdoor, please remember your passwords and PINs.

4. Third-Party Analytics, Ads & Open-Source Libraries

ScanPro PDF contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, zero crash-reporting cloud services, and zero telemetry. Crash logs, if an unexpected error ever occurs, are written strictly to a local file on your phone that you can inspect or clear in Settings.

All bundled libraries—including AndroidX Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore, CameraX, PdfBox-Android, ZXing, Coil, and bundled on-device ML Kit Text Recognition & Barcode Scanning—run locally within the offline sandbox of the application.

5. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Control

You have 100% ownership and control over every file and setting created in ScanPro PDF:

  • Delete Documents & Empty Trash: Move files to Trash or permanently delete them anytime from the Files tab, Trash screen, or Settings → Storage → Empty Trash.
  • Clear OCR & QR Caches: Use Settings → OCR → Clear cached OCR text and Settings → QR & Barcode Scanner → Clear QR scan history to wipe local recognition records immediately.
  • Strip PDF Metadata: Use the built-in PDF Info & Metadata or Redact PDF tools to strip author, title, creation timestamps, and XMP metadata before sharing a PDF.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ScanPro PDF or clearing App Storage in Android Settings permanently erases all documents, Secure Vault files, databases, and preferences stored inside the app's private storage.
