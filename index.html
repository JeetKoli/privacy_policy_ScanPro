1. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

All document and PDF tools in ScanPro PDF process your content strictly in your device's local memory and private app storage:

  • Scanned Documents & PDFs: Pages captured via camera or imported via the Android Photo Picker / Storage Access Framework (SAF) are stored inside your app's private internal directory (files/documents/) or exported only when you explicitly save or share them.
  • Offline OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Text extraction and searchable PDF generation for English (Latin) and Hindi (Devanagari) run 100% on-device. Recognized text is cached locally on your device so you can search your documents offline and can be cleared anytime in Settings → OCR → Clear cached OCR text.
  • Signatures & Redactions: Drawn or imported signatures are stored inside private app storage. True PDF Redaction permanently strips underlying text and pixels from redacted regions on-device.
  • QR & Barcode Scanner: Scanned QR codes and barcodes are decoded locally. If Save scan history is enabled, history is kept in your local Room database and can be cleared with one tap in Settings → QR & Barcode Scanner.
  • App Preferences & Language Packs: Theme settings, naming templates, and offline language preferences (15 supported languages) are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore.

2. Android Permissions We Use (Least-Privilege Policy)

ScanPro PDF follows Google Play's least-privilege permission policy and declares only the permissions required for its features and ad support:

Permission Purpose & Scope
CAMERA
(Runtime)		 Used exclusively when you open the Document Scanner, ID Card Scanner, OCR Camera, or QR/Barcode Scanner. Camera frames are processed locally on your device.
USE_BIOMETRIC
(Install-Time)		 Used exclusively with the official Android system BiometricPrompt to unlock App Lock and Secure Vault. ScanPro PDF never accesses raw biometric templates.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
(Install-Time)		 Used strictly by Google AdMob to check connectivity and load banner advertisements. Your documents and PDFs are never uploaded over the internet.
AD_ID
(Install-Time)		 Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure banner ads in compliance with Google Play policies. Reset or opt out anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
READ_MEDIA_*
(NOT Used)		 Not declared and not used. Uses zero-permission Android Photo Picker and SAF.

3. Encryption, App Lock & Hardware Secure Vault

  • Secure Vault (AES-256-GCM): Files moved into the Secure Vault are encrypted on-device using a 256-bit AES-GCM master key generated and stored inside the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • App Lock PIN Hashing: Your App Lock / Vault PIN is salted and hashed using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (120,000 iterations).
  • PDF Password Encryption: The Lock PDF tool applies standard AES encryption directly to your PDF file.
  • Offline Encrypted Backups (.scanpro-backup): Password-protected backup archives are encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Third-Party Libraries

ScanPro PDF displays non-intrusive banner ads powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect technical signals such as the device Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address (coarse location), device info, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve ads and prevent fraud. Your scanned documents, PDFs, OCR text, and Vault files are never shared with advertisers. You can manage ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads and read Google's Privacy Policy.

All document-processing libraries (AndroidX Jetpack, Room, DataStore, CameraX, PdfBox-Android, ZXing, Coil, and bundled on-device ML Kit) run locally on your device.

5. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Control

  • Delete Documents & Empty Trash: Permanently delete files anytime from Files, Trash, or Settings → Storage → Empty Trash.
  • Clear OCR & QR Caches: Wipe local OCR text cache and QR scan history anytime from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ScanPro PDF permanently erases all files, Vault items, and settings inside the app's private storage.
